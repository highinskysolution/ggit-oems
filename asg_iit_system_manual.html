📝 Section 6: How to Register New Student Candidates

Candidate Enrollment
1 Open Candidate Registration

Visit /register from the top navigation or click Register New Account on the landing page.

2 Select Department & Year

Choose department (BCA, BSc IT, or AI) and academic year (FY, SY, or TY).

3 Input Full Name & Email

Provide a unique University Roll Number (e.g. BCA202610). Set a password (min 6 chars) and complete enrollment.

🛡️ Section 7: Live Examination Room, Anti-Cheating & Auto-Submission

Assessment Integrity

⏱️ Synchronized Countdown Timer

Real-time countdown in MM:SS format with urgent color transitions at 5 minutes. The engine automatically submits all chosen answers at 00:00:00.

🎨 4-State Interactive Question Palette

Green: Answered | Yellow: Flagged for Review | Gray: Unanswered | Cyan Ring: Current Question. Instant 1-click question jumping.

🚨 AI Window Proctoring & Anti-Cheating Guard (FR-OE-05)

Active window listener detects tab switching, window blurring, minimizing, or copying text. Any violation immediately triggers a high-priority warning modal and logs the violation timestamp and count into the candidate's official transcript audit record.
